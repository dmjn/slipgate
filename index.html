SLIPGATE

A reading instrument for Quake level geometry

Slipgate ships no game data, so it reads yours. Drop a .bsp file, or drop pak0.pak / pak1.pak and pick a map from the index. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is sent anywhere; the archive is parsed here in this tab.

Quake's paks sit in the id1 folder of any Steam, GOG, or shareware install. Shareware pak0 carries the whole first episode.
